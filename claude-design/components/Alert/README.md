Inline alert at the top of the region it describes.

```jsx
<Alert variant="warning" title="Low stock">Only 3 anvils left. Restock arrives Jul 20.</Alert>
```

A soft tinted panel with a matching tinted hairline (`radius-lg`), a leading icon in the status hue, and a semibold ink title over the body. Variants: info (default, Blue), success (Green), warning (Amber), danger (Red). The icon is required — its shape keeps the status legible without colour. Never stack two per region; danger states the recovery step and is the only variant announced assertively.
